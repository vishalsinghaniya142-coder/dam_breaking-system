
# 🌊 Dam Breaking System
### AI-Powered Flood Simulation, Risk Assessment & Early Warning System

An intelligent dam-break simulation and flood risk assessment platform designed to analyze dam failure scenarios, simulate downstream flood propagation, identify vulnerable regions, and support faster disaster preparedness and decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Dam Breaking System is a technology-driven solution that combines simulation, geospatial visualization, and data analysis to understand the potential consequences of dam failure.

The platform aims to transform complex hydrological data into actionable insights through interactive visualization, flood propagation modeling, and risk assessment.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌊 **Dam Break Simulation** — Simulate dam breach scenarios and downstream flood propagation.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Geospatial Visualization** — Visualize flood-affected regions on interactive maps.
- 📊 **Flood Analytics Dashboard** — Monitor water levels, flood intensity, simulation results, and critical parameters.
- ⚠️ **Risk Assessment** — Identify vulnerable areas and analyze potential flood impact.
- 🏘️ **Affected Area Identification** — Highlight settlements and regions potentially exposed to flooding.
- ⏱️ **Flood Arrival Time Estimation** — Analyze estimated flood-wave travel time across downstream locations.
- 📈 **Data Visualization** — Present complex simulation outputs through interactive charts and visual reports.
- 🛡️ **Disaster Preparedness Support** — Provide useful insights for planning evacuation and emergency response.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Improve understanding of dam-break flood scenarios.
- Support flood hazard mapping and risk analysis.
- Enable data-driven disaster preparedness.
- Visualize complex scientific results through an accessible interface.
- Assist authorities and communities in understanding potential downstream impacts.
